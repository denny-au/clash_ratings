Clash Ratings How it all works
1How is MR worked out?

MR (Member Rating) is one score that adds up three things from this month:

1per troop donated
25per raid attack
50per war star

War stars count from regular wars and Clan War League, and they scale with the target: half value (25) against a town hall 2+ levels lower, up to 1.5× (75) against one 2+ levels higher. Map position is ignored on purpose because matchmaking can hand out unfair numbers.

2Why just these three?

They're what almost everyone does week in, week out, the whole clan can see them in-game, and they're hard to fake: an attack either landed stars or it didn't, a donation either happened or it didn't.

3What about Clan Games and events?

The game's API doesn't share them, so there's nothing to pull. The three above already cover what matters most to a clan.
